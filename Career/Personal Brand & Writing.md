## Overview
Your personal brand is how you present yourself professionally. Strong 個性 (こせい - personality/character) combined with excellent communication creates opportunities and opens doors.

## Building Your Personal Brand

### Core Elements
Your personal brand combines three key elements:
1. **Your Talents** - What you're naturally good at
2. **Your Values** - What matters to you professionally  
3. **Your Unique Selling Point (USP)** - What sets you apart

### Crafting Your USP
Your USP should appear in:
- **CV Profile Section** - Brief 2-3 line summary
- **Application Personal Statements** - Opening paragraph
- **Social Media Bios** - LinkedIn, professional platforms
- **Interview Introductions** - "Tell me about yourself"

#### Example USP
> "Hardworking and dedicated life scientist keen to pursue a career in clinical embryology. Eager to combine my Biomedical Science degree and Medical Research Council-funded laboratory experience with people-skills developed in various customer-facing student jobs. My aim is to work for an organization dedicated to improving lives and in a hospital environment working with people from a wide variety of professions."

**What makes this strong:**
- ✅ Specific career goal (clinical embryology)
- ✅ Relevant qualifications (Biomedical Science)
- ✅ Unique experience (MRC-funded research)
- ✅ Transferable skills (customer service)
- ✅ Clear values (improving lives)
- ✅ Work environment preference (hospital, diverse teams)

## Professional Writing Principles

### Writing Style Guidelines
**Do:**
- Use **professional terminology** appropriate for the organization
- Choose **proactive action words**: contributed, influenced, managed, negotiated
- Use **clear, plain language** - avoid jargon unless industry-specific
- Be **specific and measurable** where possible

**Avoid:**
- **Overuse of "I"** - becomes repetitive and self-focused
- **Overly emotional language** - "passionate," "love" (use "enthusiastic," "committed")
- **Clichés** - "think outside the box," "team player"
- **Vague statements** - be specific about achievements

### Power Words for Career Writing

#### Achievement Words
| Category | Strong Words | Weak Alternatives |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Leadership** | Directed, orchestrated, spearheaded | Helped, worked on |
| **Problem-Solving** | Resolved, diagnosed, streamlined | Fixed, did |
| **Growth** | Expanded, accelerated, maximized | Increased, made better |
| **Innovation** | Pioneered, developed, transformed | Changed, improved |
| **Communication** | Presented, articulated, facilitated | Talked, said |

#### Specific Examples
```
❌ Weak: "Helped customers with their problems"
✅ Strong: "Resolved customer inquiries, achieving 98% satisfaction rating"

❌ Weak: "Was responsible for social media"  
✅ Strong: "Managed social media strategy, increasing engagement by 150%"

❌ Weak: "Good at working in teams"
✅ Strong: "Collaborated with cross-functional teams to deliver projects 20% ahead of schedule"
```

## CV Writing Mastery

### Structure and Content
Your CV should tell a compelling story of progression and achievement:

#### Essential Sections
1. **Contact Information** - Phone, email, LinkedIn, location
2. **Professional Profile** - 2-3 lines summarizing your value proposition
3. **Key Skills** - Technical and soft skills relevant to target role
4. **Professional Experience** - Reverse chronological, achievement-focused
5. **Education** - Degrees, certifications, relevant coursework
6. **Additional Sections** - Projects, publications, languages, volunteer work

#### Professional Experience Format
```
Job Title | Company Name | Dates
• Achievement-focused bullet point with quantifiable result
• Action verb + task + measurable outcome
• Problem solved + method + impact

Example:
Marketing Coordinator | TechStart Inc. | Jan 2022 - Present  
• Increased social media engagement by 200% through targeted content strategy
• Managed email campaigns reaching 10,000+ subscribers with 25% open rate
• Collaborated with sales team to generate 50+ qualified leads monthly
```

### ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Optimization

#### What is ATS?
Modern companies use software to scan CVs before humans see them. Your CV must be both human-readable and ATS-friendly.

#### ATS-Friendly Formatting
**Do:**
- Use standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills)
- Include keywords from job description
- Use simple, clean formatting
- Save as .docx or .pdf (check job posting preference)
- Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman)

**Avoid:**
- Tables, text boxes, graphics
- Unusual fonts or formatting
- Headers/footers with critical information
- Images or logos
- Fancy design elements

#### Keyword Strategy
```
Job Description Says: "Project management experience required"
Your CV Should Include: "Project management," "managed projects," "project coordination"

Job Description Says: "Proficient in Python programming"  
Your CV Should Include: "Python," "Python programming," "software development"
```

## Digital Footprint Improvement

### LinkedIn Optimization
**Complete Profile Elements:**
- **Professional headline** - Not just job title, include value proposition
- **About section** - Expanded version of CV profile with personality
- **Experience descriptions** - Mirror CV but can be more conversational
- **Skills endorsements** - Get colleagues to endorse relevant skills
- **Recommendations** - Request from managers, colleagues, clients

#### LinkedIn Headline Examples
```
❌ Generic: "Software Developer at ABC Company"
✅ Compelling: "Full-Stack Developer | Building scalable web applications | Python, React, AWS"

❌ Basic: "Marketing Professional"  
✅ Specific: "Digital Marketing Specialist | Driving growth through data-driven campaigns | SEO, PPC, Analytics"
```

## Communication Best Practices

### Written Communication
**Email Etiquette:**
- **Subject lines** - Clear and specific ("Follow-up: Marketing Coordinator Interview")
- **Greeting** - Professional but warm ("Dear Ms. Johnson" or "Hello John")
- **Body** - Concise, structured, action-oriented
- **Closing** - Appropriate sign-off ("Best regards," "Sincerely")

**Application Letters:**
- **Research** - Show knowledge of company and role
- **Customization** - Tailor each application  
- **Value proposition** - What you bring, not what you want
- **Call to action** - Request interview or next steps

### Verbal Communication
**Phone/Video Interviews:**
- **Environment** - Quiet, professional background
- **Technology** - Test camera, microphone, internet beforehand
- **Preparation** - Practice common questions out loud
- **Follow-up** - Send thank-you email within 24 hours

## Personal Brand Consistency

### Across All Platforms
Ensure your professional identity is consistent across:
- **CV/Resume** - Formal, achievement-focused
- **LinkedIn** - Professional but personable
- **Portfolio Website** - Showcase work and personality
- **Email Communications** - Professional and responsive
- **Interview Presentation** - Authentic but polished

### Authenticity Balance
```
🎭 Professional Persona ←→ 🧑 Authentic Self

Sweet Spot: Professional authenticity
- Real personality shines through
- Values and interests align with career
- Honest about strengths and growth areas
- Comfortable being yourself in professional settings
```

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### CV/Application Errors
- **Typos and grammar mistakes** - Always proofread multiple times
- **Inconsistent formatting** - Use same fonts, spacing, bullet styles
- **Missing contact information** - Include phone and email prominently
- **Irrelevant information** - Focus on what matters for target role
- **Lack of quantifiable achievements** - Include numbers wherever possible

### Digital Presence Issues
- **Outdated information** - Regular updates across all platforms
- **Inappropriate content** - Review social media privacy settings
- **Incomplete profiles** - Fill out all relevant sections
- **Generic messaging** - Customize connection requests and applications

Your personal brand is your professional 名刺 (めいし - business card) to the world. Invest time in crafting it thoughtfully, and opportunities will follow!
