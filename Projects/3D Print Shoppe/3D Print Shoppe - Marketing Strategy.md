# 3D Print Shoppe - Marketing Strategy

## Platform Priority Stack

| Rank | Platform | Why | Effort | Status |
|------|----------|-----|--------|--------|
| 1 | Nextdoor | Hyper-local, exact target audience (WFH homeowners) | Low | Ready to launch |
| 2 | NTB Display | Passive lead gen, car enthusiast audience | Low | Active |
| 3 | Instagram | Visual products, Reels for organic reach | Medium | Winter break |
| 4 | Facebook Marketplace/Groups | Local buy/sell, community groups | Low | Post-launch |
| 5 | TikTok | Only if making videos (timelapses, reveals) | High | Maybe later |

**Skip:** LinkedIn (B2B, not my market), Twitter/X (not local-focused), Pinterest (low conversion for custom work)

---

## Nextdoor Strategy

### Optimal Timing
- **Best days:** Thursday or Friday
- **Best time:** 5-7pm local (WFH folks winding down)
- **Frequency:** Every 2 weeks (engagement dies after ~14 days)

### Posting Schedule
| Week | Post | Angle |
|------|------|-------|
| 1 (Launch) | Shoji Lamp | "Student pivot" - honesty about being new |
| 3 | Shoe Rack | "WFH problem solver" - functional utility |
| 5 | Great Wave Panel | "Local access" - art/hueforge capability |
| 7+ | Rotate new products | Keep it fresh |

### Tone Guidelines
- Sound like typing on phone after dinner
- Slightly rambling, personal, natural
- End with question or invitation to reply
- NO exclamation spam
- NO corporate language
