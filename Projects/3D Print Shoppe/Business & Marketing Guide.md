## Pricing Strategy

### Tiered Pricing Model

```
Small Items ($8-15):
- Print time: 1-3 hours
- Examples: Ornaments, keychains, small decor
- Material cost: $1-2
- Margin: 75-85%

Medium Items ($15-30):
- Print time: 3-8 hours
- Examples: Planters, organizers, medium decor
- Material cost: $2-5
- Margin: 70-80%

Large Items ($30-50+):
- Print time: 8+ hours
- Examples: Wall art, large planters, custom pieces
- Material cost: $5-10
- Margin: 65-75%

Add-ons:
- Rush order: +$5 (1-2 day turnaround)
- Custom design: +$10-20 (design time)
- Bulk discount: 15-20% off for 5+ identical items
```

### Cost Analysis

**Per-Print Costs:**
- Filament: $0.02-0.03/gram (PLA)
- Electricity: $0.10-0.20/hour (A1 printer)
- Time: $0 (passive printing)

**Fixed Costs (Monthly):**
- Domain: $1.25/month (your-domain.com)
- Hosting: $0 (Vercel free tier)
- Formspree: $0 (50 forms/month free)
- Marketing: $0 (organic Nextdoor)
- **Total: ~$1.50/month**

**Break-Even:**
- Need ~1 sale per month to cover costs
- Target: 15-20 sales/month = $300-400 revenue

## Sales Process

### Customer Journey

**1. Discovery (Nextdoor/Website)**
- User sees post or finds via search
- Browses vendor catalogs or showcases

**2. Inquiry (Form/DM)**
- User submits quote request or DMs
- Includes item, color, quantity, timeline

**3. Quote (24hr Response)**
- Send itemized quote via email
- Include: price, colors available, pickup dates
- Payment info: PayPal/Venmo/CashApp/Cash

**4. Confirmation (Payment)**
- Customer sends payment (50% deposit for custom)
- Send confirmation email
- Add to print queue

**5. Production (1-4 days)**
- Print item
- Quality check
- Photograph finished piece

**6. Pickup (Weekend/Appointment)**
- Send "ready for pickup" email
- Coordinate time/location
- Collect remaining payment (if deposit)

**7. Follow-up (3 days post-pickup)**
- Send thank you email
- Request review/photo
- Offer 10% off next order

## Risk Management

### Business Risks

**Low Demand:**
- Mitigation: Diversify marketing (not just Nextdoor)
- Backup: Focus on B2B (consistent orders)
- Timeline: Re-evaluate after 3 months

**Printer Failure:**
- Mitigation: Warranty coverage (1 year)
- Backup: Makerspace rental for urgent orders
- Prevention: Regular maintenance

**Competition:**
- Mitigation: Focus on service (local, fast, custom)
- Differentiation: Personal touch vs online shops
- Pricing: Stay competitive but not cheapest

**Seasonal Slumps:**
- Mitigation: Holiday focus (Oct-Dec)
- Backup: Subscription model for regulars
- Planning: Stock prints during slow times

### Legal Considerations

**Business Registration:**
- Not required for <$10k annual revenue in VA
- Track expenses for tax deductions
- Consider LLC if revenue >$20k/year

**Intellectual Property:**
- Only use CC0/commercial-license designs
- Verify license before printing
- Attribute creators when required

**Liability:**
- Disclaim: "Not for children under 3" (choking hazard)
- No food-contact items (unless food-safe filament)
- No medical devices or load-bearing parts

**Taxes:**
- Track all revenue (spreadsheet)
- Deduct: filament, electricity, domain, repairs
- File quarterly if self-employed income >$1000

## Lessons & Best Practices

### What Works

**Marketing:**
- Showcase posts > catalog posts (higher engagement)
- Pricing transparency (ranges, not "contact for quote")
- Personal touch (local neighbor, not corporate)
- Visual quality (good photos essential)

**Pricing:**
- Ranges work better than fixed prices
- Rush fees filter serious buyers
- Bulk discounts encourage larger orders
- Round numbers easier to remember

**Operations:**
- Weekend pickup reduces complexity
- Quote system allows pricing flexibility
- 24hr response time expected
- Payment upfront prevents no-shows

### What Doesn't Work

**Marketing:**
- Full catalog posts (overwhelming)
- Generic copy ("Great quality!")
- Inconsistent posting (need regular presence)
- Ignoring comments (engagement drops)

**Pricing:**
- Too low (devalues service)
- Hidden fees (frustrates customers)
- No rush option (miss urgent orders)

**Operations:**
- Same-day turnaround (unrealistic)
- No order confirmation (confusion)
- Cash-only (limits customers)
