## 💡 Key Decisions Made

### Business Model
- **90% catalog / 10% custom** - Pre-curated designs + simple modifications
- **No CAD services** - Use existing MakerWorld designs
- **Local focus** - Neighborhood pickup/delivery only initially
- **Side hustle** - Keep as supplementary income, max 3 printers

### Pricing Strategy
- **$20-50 sweet spot** - Premium positioning vs Amazon imports
- **Bundles at 15-20% off** - Encourages larger orders
- **50% deposits** for custom orders - Troll protection
- **Seasonal adjustments** - Holiday premiums, January value bundles

### Platform Strategy
- **Nextdoor primary** - Where target customers are
- **Website secondary** - Professional presence, better control
- **No Etsy initially** - Focus on local first
- **B2B prioritized** - Higher margins, repeat business
