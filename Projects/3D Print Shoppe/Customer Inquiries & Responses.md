## Customer Service Guidelines

### Response Time Targets
- **Initial inquiry:** <24 hours
- **Quote requests:** <24 hours
- **Questions during order:** <12 hours
- **Order ready notification:** Same day

### Communication Limits
- **Maximum 2 back-and-forth messages** before requesting clarity
- If customer can't decide after 3 exchanges → politely defer
- Complex requests → offer phone/video call (text only)

### Red Flags 🚩
- New Nextdoor account (<3 months)
- Vague requests + price haggling
- Refuses deposit for custom work
- "How cheap can you go?" questions
- Ghosts after quote, returns with urgency

**Action:** Require 100% upfront or politely decline
