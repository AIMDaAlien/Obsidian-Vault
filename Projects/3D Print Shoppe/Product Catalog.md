## Pricing Formula

```
Base Cost = Material (PLA @ $20/kg) + Time ($0/hr passive)
Material example: 50g print = $1 material cost

Target margin: 80-85% for small items
Target margin: 60-70% for large items

Small ($12-18): 20-50g material, 3-5 hrs print
Medium ($20-30): 50-100g material, 5-8 hrs print  
Large ($35-50): 100-200g material, 8-12 hrs print
```

## Print Time Optimization

**Overnight runs (11 hrs):**
- 2 medium items
- 1 large item
- 3-4 small items

**Weekend batch printing:**
- Print 6-8 stock items
- Friday 6 PM → Monday 6 AM = 60 hours capacity
