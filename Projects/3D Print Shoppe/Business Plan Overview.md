# Business Plan Overview

## Business Model
**Name:** Aim's 3D Print Shoppe  
**Launch:** October 2025  
**Model:** 90% Catalog + 10% Custom

### Value Proposition
- **Local:** Pick up in neighborhood or $5 delivery
- **Customizable:** Your design, your colors, your way
- **Eco-friendly:** Biodegradable PLA from corn starch
- **Personal:** Direct communication with maker

## Target Market
**Primary:** Suburban millennials and boomers (35-65 age range)
**Platform:** Nextdoor (100M users, neighborhood-focused)
**Geography:** Local suburban community

### Customer Segments
1. **Home organizers** - WFH setups, desk accessories
2. **Gift buyers** - Holiday, birthdays, personalized items
3. **Plant enthusiasts** - Custom planters, eco-conscious
4. **Practical fixers** - Replacement parts, phone stands
5. **B2B clients** - Bulk orders (Stephanie V - elderly gifts)

## Pricing Strategy
**Sweet Spot:** $20-50 (premium vs Amazon $5-20)

| Size | Price Range | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Small | $12-18 | Coasters, keychains |
| Medium | $20-30 | Planters, organizers |
| Large | $35-50 | Wall art, large decor |
| Custom | $30-75 | Client designs, scaling |

**Bundles:** 15-20% discount for 3-5 item sets  
**Seasonal:** +10-20% holidays, value bundles post-New Year

## Payment Protection
- **In-stock items:** 100% upfront payment
- **Custom orders:** 50% deposit to start, 50% at pickup
- **No refunds** once printing starts (stated upfront)
- **Methods:** PayPal, Cash App (Venmo backup)

## Financial Projections
| Month | Revenue Target | Strategy |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| 1 | $300 | Validation, 12-15 sales |
| 2 | $400 | Refinement, drop losers |
| 3 | $500 | B2B wholesale added |
| 4 | $600 | Evaluate 2nd printer |

**ROI Target:** 4 months  
**Initial Investment:** ~$130 (tools, supplies, marketing)  
**Equipment on hand:** Bambu Lab A1 Combo + 8kg filament

## Operational Constraints
**Time:** 60-90 min/day (spontaneous schedule)  
**Production:** ~3-4 jobs/day max (one printer)  
**Hours:** No printing past 11 PM (neighbors)  
**Space:** Bedroom setup, can add 2 more printers max

## Distribution Channels
1. **Website** - aim3dprints.com (primary)
2. **Nextdoor** - Community posts, business page
3. **Local partnerships** - Cafe, mosque (planned)
4. **Etsy** - Future passive channel (Month 3+)

## Competitive Advantages
1. **No local 3D printing competitors** on Nextdoor
2. **Fast turnaround** - 3-5 days vs 1-2 weeks online
3. **Personal service** - Work directly with maker
4. **Eco positioning** - Biodegradable material messaging
5. **Local pickup** - Zero shipping cost/wait

## Key Metrics to Track
- Orders per week
- Average order value
- Profit margin per item
- Printer utilization %
- Customer acquisition cost
- Repeat customer rate
- Nextdoor engagement (views, comments)

## Risk Management
**Low-Risk Moves:**
- Starting on Nextdoor (free)
- Using existing equipment
- Testing niches before committing
- Keeping day job while building

**Avoid:**
- Quitting income before $1,500+/month proven
- Buying inventory before demand validated
- Competing on price with online vendors
- Taking loans or debt

## Success Criteria
**6-Month Goal:** $800-1,200/month sustainable side income  
**Long-term Vision:** 3 printers max, stay as side hustle  
**Exit Strategy:** Keep as passive income, not scale to full business
