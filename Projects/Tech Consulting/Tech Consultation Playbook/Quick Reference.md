# Quick Reference - Key Learnings

#quick-reference #cheatsheet

## M3 Expressive in 60 Seconds

**Shape**: 4dp → 56dp scale, asymmetric corners (32px 32px 12px 32px)  
**Motion**: `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` always  
**Size**: Massive scale (9xl headings, py-6 buttons)  
**Borders**: 3-4px minimum (not 1px)  
**Hover**: Transform radius + scale (24px → 32px, scale 1.08)

## Critical Don'ts

❌ Linear easing (use spring physics)  
❌ 1px borders (use 3-4px)  
❌ Hourly pricing (use flat rates)  
❌ Corporate voice (use student/local)  
❌ Forgetting follow-up call (1 week)
