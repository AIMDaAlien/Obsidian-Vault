# Tools & Client Workflow

## Tech Stack (All Free)
**Intake:** Google Forms
**Scheduling:** Calendly (block prayer times: Fajr 5:30-6am, Dhuhr 12:30-1:30pm, Asr 3:30-4:30pm, Maghrib sunset, Isha 7-8pm)
**Payment:** Cash, Venmo, PayPal, CashApp (after service)
**Parts Research:** PCPartPicker, Logical Increments
**Deliverables:** Google Sheets/Docs, Canva (PDFs)
**Communication:** Text-only (protects time vs calls)

## Client Workflow (Text-Based)

### Step 1: Form Submission
Client fills Google Form → you get email notification

### Step 2: Initial Contact (within 4 hours)
```
Hi [NAME], this is [owner] - thanks for filling out the PC setup form!

Quick clarification: [1-2 specific questions based on form]

Once I understand your needs, I can give you a quote and we'll schedule a time for me to come by. Usually takes about 2 hours.

What's your availability this week?
```

### Step 3: Booking Confirmation
```
Perfect! I can do [DAY] at [TIME].

Service: PC Setup - $175
Included: [list 3-4 items]
Payment: Cash/Venmo/PayPal after service

I'll text you 2 hours before to confirm. Sound good?
```

### Step 4: Day-Before Reminder
```
Hey [NAME]! Reminder: I'll be there tomorrow at [TIME] for your PC setup. Should take about 2 hours. See you then!
```

### Step 5: Post-Service Follow-Up (7 days later)
```
Hey [NAME]! Hope your [computer] is working well. Would you mind leaving a quick testimonial about your experience? It really helps a small business like mine. Thanks!

[Google Form testimonial link]
```

## Prayer Time Handling

**Calendly setup:** 30-min buffer slots around each prayer time

**If prayer hits during appointment:**
> "I need to take a 10-minute break at [time] for a personal commitment. Would you prefer we pause and resume, or schedule the remaining portion for tomorrow?"

**Professional = 90% respect this. Anyone who doesn't = not your client.**
