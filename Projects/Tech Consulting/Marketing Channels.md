# Marketing Channels & Strategy

## Primary Channels (Priority Order)

### 1. Dad's Network ⭐ FASTEST
**Why:** Warm leads, trusted referral source, daily customer contact
**Action:** Give dad phone number cards, simple script
**Expected ROI:** 2-3 clients in first 3 weeks

### 2. Google Business Profile
**Setup:** 15 minutes, free, permanent
**Shows in:** "computer help near me" searches
**Optimization:** Upload photos, respond to questions, request reviews
**Expected ROI:** 1-2 organic leads/month after 2 months

### 3. Facebook Marketplace
**Post as:** "Item for sale" (workaround for services)
**Price:** List as $175 or $1 (say "from $85" in description)
**Repost:** Every 7 days by editing + reposting
**Join:** 2-3 local groups ([City] Community, [ZIP] Buy/Sell)

### 4. Nextdoor
**Strategy:** Wait 10-14 days after 3D printing post
**Post with:** Testimonials + social proof
**Alternative:** Comment on tech-related threads organically
**Consider:** Second business page (but NOT immediately)

## Channels to Skip
❌ **Upwork/Fiverr** - 20% fees, race to bottom pricing, months to build ratings
❌ **Paid ads** - Wait until Month 3+ when you know cost-per-client
❌ **Yelp** - Too competitive for new businesses
❌ **TaskRabbit** - 15% cut, not worth it

## Content Strategy (Month 2+)
**Simple blog posts on your site:**
- "Gaming PC vs Prebuilt: What's Actually Worth It"
- "5 Things to Check Before Buying Any Laptop"
- "Why Your New PC Needs Proper Setup"

**Goal:** Answer questions → rank in Google → build authority

## Portfolio Leverage
**Link teardown.cafe in About section:**
> "When I'm not helping clients, I document device teardowns and builds at teardown.cafe - check it out to see my approach to technical projects."

**Why this works:** Proves you don't just talk about tech - you build enterprise-grade systems.
