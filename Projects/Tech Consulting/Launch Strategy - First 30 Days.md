# Launch Strategy - First 30 Days

## Week 1: Foundation (Day 1-7)
- [ ] Create Google Form intake (15 min)
- [ ] Set up Calendly with prayer time blocks (20 min)
- [ ] Test both with dummy submission (5 min)
- [ ] Take 2-3 service photos
- [ ] Talk to [family member] - give him script + phone cards
- [ ] Post on Facebook Marketplace as "item for sale"

**[family member]'s Script:**
> "Hey, random thing - my son does tech consulting now. If you ever need help setting up a new computer or figuring out which one to buy, he's really good at explaining things in normal language. Just thought I'd mention it!"

## Week 2: Bridge Campaign
**Goal:** 2-3 clients from existing network at $150 (40% off testimonial discount)

**Text blast to past PC help recipients:**
> "Hey [Name]! Remember when I helped you with your PC? I'm officially launching tech consultation - doing new PC setups for $150 this month (first 3 clients). If you or anyone you know needs help, I'd love to work with you. Book here: [calendly]"

**Offer:** First 3 clients get 40% off in exchange for:
- Detailed testimonial
- Permission for case study
- Video testimonial (optional but gold)

## Week 3-4: Documentation Phase
- Document time spent per service type
- Take before/after photos (bloatware screenshots, etc.)
- Request testimonials 7 days after service
- Create simple PDF template for Service 1
- Track lead sources ([family member] vs Facebook vs other)

## Month 2 Goals
- Add Service 1 (consultation) to offerings
- Create simple one-page website with testimonials
- Raise prices to standard ($175/$100)
- Post on Nextdoor with social proof

## Month 3 Goals
- Add Service 2 (parts list) if demand exists
- Consider partnerships (local computer shops, real estate agents)
- Evaluate time/revenue ratio - keep what's profitable

---

## Critical Timeline Insight
**Realistic money timeline:**
- Week 1: Setup (no revenue)
- Week 2: First inquiry (2-3 days to convert)
- Week 3: First payment received
- Week 4+: Steady bookings

**Don't expect income in first 10 days.** Service businesses need time to build trust.
