For a low-powered, yet ample performance small form factor home server, you'll want a balance between energy efficiency, processing power, and storage capacity. Here's a suggested configuration:

1. **Processor (CPU):** 
   - Intel Core i3 or AMD Ryzen 3 processors are energy-efficient and provide sufficient power for your needs.

2. **Memory (RAM):** 
   - 8 GB to 16 GB of DDR 4 RAM should be ample for running websites, smart home applications, and a version control system.

3. **Storage:**
   - **Primary Storage:** A 256 GB to 512 GB SSD (NVMe if possible) for faster boot times and improved application performance.
   - **Secondary Storage:** A larger HDD (1 TB or more) for data storage and backups.

4. **Motherboard:**
   - Look for a micro-ATX or mini-ITX motherboard with integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth capabilities for smart home device connectivity.

5. **Case:**
   - Choose a small form factor case that supports mini-ITX motherboards and has good airflow for cooling. Ensure it can accommodate your chosen storage drives.

6. **Power Supply:**
   - A 300 W to 450 W power supply should be sufficient for this low-power setup.

7. **Cooling:**
   - Stock CPU cooler should be sufficient for the processor's power requirements in this setup.

8. **Operating System:**
   - Consider using a lightweight Linux distribution like Ubuntu Server or Debian for efficient resource usage.

9. **Networking:**
   - Gigabit Ethernet is essential for stable and fast network connectivity. Wi-Fi can be helpful if your server needs to be placed away from the router.

10. **Remote Access and Security:**
    - Configure secure remote access using SSH, and consider setting up a VPN for additional security.

11. **Backup Solutions:**
    - Set up automated backups to an external drive or cloud storage for data protection.

12. **Energy Efficiency:**
    - Look for components that are known for their energy efficiency to keep power consumption low.

This configuration should provide a good balance between performance, energy efficiency, and space-saving design. It's important to note that the specific components you choose will depend on availability and your budget, so make sure to research the latest options and reviews before making your purchases. This type of server should be capable of handling your web hosting, version control, and smart home needs without using excessive power or taking up too much space.
