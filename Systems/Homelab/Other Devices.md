There are several other devices, beyond microcontrollers and Raspberry Pi boards, that are featureful and well-suited for learning and experimentation in the fields of IoT and operating systems. Here are some options:

1. **BeagleBone:**
   - BeagleBone boards, like the BeagleBone Black, offer more advanced features and capabilities compared to Raspberry Pi. They are suitable for exploring IoT and operating system concepts.

2. **NVIDIA Jetson Series:**
   - The NVIDIA Jetson series, such as Jetson Nano and Jetson Xavier, are designed for AI and robotics applications. They provide powerful processing capabilities for edge computing and AI experimentation.

3. **UDOO x 86:**
   - UDOO x 86 combines the power of a PC with the versatility of an Arduino board. It's great for IoT and robotics projects, offering x 86 processing and compatibility.

4. **Intel Edison and Galileo:**
   - These Intel boards are designed for IoT development. They offer features like built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, making them suitable for wireless projects.

5. **Orange Pi:**
   - Similar to Raspberry Pi, Orange Pi boards come with various models and features. They are suitable for IoT projects, as well as learning about operating systems and embedded systems.

6. **Particle Devices:**
   - Particle devices like Photon, Electron, and Argon are specifically designed for IoT applications. They offer cloud connectivity and are excellent for building connected projects.

7. **STM 32 Discovery Boards:**
   - STM 32 Discovery boards are ARM-based microcontroller boards that are widely used in embedded systems development and IoT projects.

8. **Arduino MKR Series:**
   - Arduino MKR boards are designed for IoT and battery-powered projects. They are compact and energy-efficient, making them suitable for remote sensing applications.

9. **ESP 8266 and ESP 32 Modules:**
   - While often used as microcontrollers, these modules are powerful enough to run lightweight operating systems and experiment with IoT concepts.

10. **RISC-V Development Boards:**
    - RISC-V is an open-source instruction set architecture. Boards like HiFive and Longan Nano allow you to explore this architecture and its potential for operating systems.

11. **MicroBit:**
    - MicroBit is a compact and beginner-friendly platform for learning programming and IoT concepts.

12. **Pine 64 Boards:**
    - Pine 64 offers a range of single-board computers that can be used for IoT, server projects, and learning about operating systems.

When choosing a device, consider factors like processing power, connectivity options, community support, and the specific features you need for your projects. Many of these devices come with ample resources and communities that provide tutorials, documentation, and support to help you get started on your IoT and operating systems journey.
