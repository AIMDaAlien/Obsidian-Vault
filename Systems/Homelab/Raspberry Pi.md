### Useful as a [[Home Server]] in your [[Home Lab]].

1. **Desktop Replacement**: lightweight desktop for basic web browsing and coding
2. Stream media
3. Wireshark and Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)
4. **Pi-Hole** -- Creates network-wide ad and tracker blocking for all devices on the network
5. **Educational tool** -- Teaching programming, electronics, and computer science concepts
6. **Remote Control** -- Develop systems to remotely control and monitor devices like robots and smart home devices
7. **AI and Machine Learning**
8. External or Cloud storage
