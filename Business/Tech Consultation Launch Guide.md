# Tech Consultation Side Business - Launch Guide

## Overview
**Goal:** Launch local tech consultation service in Woodbridge VA (22192) area
**Timeline:** 3-month proper build (no rush)
**Target:** Suburban residents, older adults, small businesses confused by tech
**Positioning:** Student-run "tech translator" - NOT repair shop

## Core Insight
**Geographic monopoly > online competition.** Local, in-person service beats Upwork/Fiverr for urgent needs like PC setup.

## Critical Success Factors
1. **Start with ONE service** (PC Setup) before expanding
2. **Dad's network = fastest path to first clients** (assistant store manager = daily customer contact)
3. **Separate brands** (3D printing vs tech consultation - different audiences)
4. **Portfolio matters** (teardown.cafe proves technical credibility)
5. **Text-first workflow** protects time better than calls

## Revenue Reality Check
- Month 1: 3-5 clients, $500-800
- Month 2: 8-12 clients, $1200-1800
- Month 3: 12-16 clients, $2000-2800
- Viable side hustle at 3hrs/day capacity

## Key Mistake to Avoid
**Don't build website before getting first client.** Form + text workflow works TODAY. Website is Month 2 project after you have testimonials.
