# v4.5.0-alpha.1 Deployed, 5.0 Phase 1 Merged

## In plain words

The Penthouse got a new version today, 4.5.0-alpha.1. What it means if you use the app:

- The server now sees where a connection really comes from, even though all traffic passes through Cloudflare. Rate limits and abuse checks apply to the right person, and nobody can fake their address with a forged header.
- Deleted photos and files are cleaned up on a schedule, and your storage quota reflects it.
- Notifications from the same chat fold into one entry with a count instead of piling up.
- Group managers can choose a GIF rating for their group.
- You can opt out of the people directory in Settings.
- A privacy page explains what the server keeps and for how long.

Behind the scenes, the first piece of the 5.0 redesign (the visual foundations and the "release ticket" welcome screen) was merged into the 5.0 branch. Nothing from 5.0 is live yet.

One decision came out of today: profiles will soon be hidden from search by default. A hidden person can still be found by typing their whole username (small typos allowed), and anyone can switch themselves to public in Settings.

## Decisions today

- People search: profiles hidden from search by default, existing accounts included. A hidden profile is found only when the whole username is typed, allowing at most two wrong characters. Users keep a setting to show publicly or stay hidden from Discover; hidden is the default. The change gets a mention in the update notes and a one-time nudge in Settings asking whether to stay hidden or appear in Discover. Design pending (today's opt-out is visible by default, exact username as the only way in).
- Removed accounts stay in the admin member list with status `removed`; removal is a status change, not a row delete. The two smoke-test accounts from the deploy still show after removal; that is by design, and a filter is a possible follow-up.
- MetalBench, a separate project on the same domain, refused connections during the deploy checks. Not a Penthouse task.
