If you think you can meet the recruiter's requirements the next step is  to research the organization to understand what they do, their values are so that you are confident that they match your own and you can say why you chose them

Also check their social media to find out what they do online. 
 
## Consider your motivation for wanting to apply

-   Values / ethics / culture
-   Core business activities it is involved in
-   Clear opportunities for career progression
-   Size of organization
-   Induction and training program
-   Forward thinking - does the organization have a reputation for                          implementing new ideas? 
-   Flexible working - can staff work flexible hours?
-   Community engagement - does it get involved in local / charitable events?
