### The five ways to answer it

1. **I am the problem solver** 
     Because you want to answer the employer's main concerns. The applicant should address how they can help the employer solve a problem that they have not articulated, but the applicant perceived. 

2.  **The sales pitch** 
      You are, after all, selling yourself in a job interview. "A candidate should have a well said, unique and structured 30 to 45 second elevator pitch with a brief introduction of self, work conducted, work desired and a selling point as to how to deliver results." 
      A good example being:  "I think I would excel at this job. Here's why..."

3. **Tell a story**
      Past performance situations often predicts futures and how one's experience will address a primary facet of the role being fulfilled. Whether or not you offer up an elevator pitch, cite a specific example or two of prior work you've done an d the results to demonstrate what you can do for your future employer.
      "i think you should hire me because I've been searching for this job for as long as i can remember. I have an interesting story about that in fact..."     And then launch into your story

4. **Heavy on specifics**
     You could do an info dump. 
     - *Know the specifics of the company and how you would fit in*
     - Offer up *specific and unique skills*
     - Offer up *specific accomplishments*
      A good answer would be like:  
      *"I think I have a strong track record of doing well at my job, and I think I can bring the same succeess in this position as well. Back at my old position..."* 
        Then you offer stats or specific examples of what youve accomplished. It should make you seem very prepared for the interview.

5. **'Because This Is Such a Good Fit for Me'**
      Companies want their candidates to want their job
      "You should hire me because I want to make a difference in your company. I have the necessary skills to be successful and i want to learn and grow with this company, and your job is the one that fits me best."
