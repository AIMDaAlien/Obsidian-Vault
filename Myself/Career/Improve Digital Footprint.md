
You can also develop a professional profile online, which demonstrates your skills and experiences to potential recruiters. LinkedIn is a common way to do it but there are others like twitter, blogging google+ or facebook. 

You can draw attention to your online cv by joining groups relevant to the kinds of jobs that interest you and join online group discussions to build reputation as someone genuinely interested. 

## Job / work experience hunting

-   You can look at job adverts placed by individual organizations on LinkedIn™.
-   Research individual organizations on LinkedIn™ who might be offering jobs or where you might enquire about jobs.
-   Contact individuals on LinkedIn™ to try to organize work shadowing or work experience.
